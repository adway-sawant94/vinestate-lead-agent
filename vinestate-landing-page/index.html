OUR METHODOLOGY

Onboarding to Delivery

A structured, 8-step journey built to transition your business operations into a high-performance growth system.

01

Discovery Call

We start with a 30–45 minute consultation (at no cost) to understand your business operations, current roadblocks, and primary revenue goals.

02

Business Audit

Our team reviews your website, Google presence, social channels, and current lead handling systems to identify key growth and conversion opportunities.

03

Custom Proposal

Based on our audit findings, we recommend the right package/retainer tier with no upselling. No upselling beyond your real needs.

04

Onboarding & Access

Once approved, we collect your brand assets, business copy guidelines, and set up shared permissions to launch development seamlessly.

05

Build Phase

We build your website, branding structures, CRMs, quotation workflows, and AI concierge bots. You receive milestone updates to keep us aligned.

06

Testing & Training

We run end-to-end tests on every element (forms, notifications, chatbots) and train your internal team to manage the leads and analytics dashboards.

07

Launch

Your growth system goes live. We monitor integrations in real-time and provide 30 days of dedicated post-launch technical support.

08

Ongoing Growth (Retainer)

For retainer clients, we transit into monthly execution: search SEO, targeted ads, content direction, and chatbot updates, backed by live monthly reporting.
